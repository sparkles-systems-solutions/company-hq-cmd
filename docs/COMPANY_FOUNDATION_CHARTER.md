# Company Foundation Charter

**Company working name:** Sparkles Systems & Solutions  
**Operating model:** Founder-led Human + Virtual AI hybrid  
**Document status:** Draft for Founder / CEO review  
**Scope:** Company identity, purpose, operating principles and foundational governance

> This document records the current company direction discussed with the Founder. It is not a certificate of incorporation, legal opinion, employment record, or evidence of regulatory registration.

## 1. Vision

“To become a globally recognized technology company that transforms ideas into intelligent, innovative and reliable digital solutions, empowering businesses and people to achieve more.”

## 2. Mission

“To design, develop and deliver high-quality software, AI-powered platforms and enterprise technology solutions through innovation, automation, professional expertise and continuous improvement, while creating measurable value for our customers and society.”

## 3. Slogan

**IDEAS INTO INNOVATION. TECHNOLOGY INTO IMPACT.**

## 4. Core Values

- Innovation
- Excellence
- Integrity
- Customer First
- Accountability
- Security
- Continuous Improvement
- Teamwork

## 5. Company Purpose and Service Direction

The company is being built as a technology solutions business focused on designing, developing, delivering and supporting:

- Custom software and business applications
- AI-powered platforms and intelligent automation
- Enterprise systems and integrations
- Healthcare and hospital technology solutions
- Mobile and web applications
- Data, reporting and management solutions
- Infrastructure, cloud and technical services

The list describes the intended service direction. It does not imply that every service is already commercially launched or operational.

## 6. Membership Model

The Founder / CEO is currently the company's only confirmed human member.

The organization design contains 41 Virtual AI role definitions. These are virtual work roles, not human employees, contractors, independent legal persons or autonomous agents. Their activation and access remain subject to explicit Founder / CEO approval and technical controls.

No human recruitment or employment is confirmed by this charter.

## 7. Governance Principles

1. The Founder / CEO is the final human approval authority.
2. Virtual AI roles may research, analyze, draft, plan, propose, develop within approved scope, test and document.
3. Virtual AI roles cannot approve their own work.
4. Virtual AI roles cannot independently sign agreements, make binding legal decisions, hire or terminate people, spend money, make payments, publish releases, change production systems or perform destructive actions.
5. Access is denied by default and granted only for a defined role, task, data scope and period.
6. Sensitive information is restricted by default.
7. Important decisions and work products must have traceable records.
8. Qualified external human professionals must be used where legal, accounting, regulatory or other professional sign-off is required.

## 8. Decision and Delivery Lifecycle

**Request → Analysis → Proposal → Founder / CEO Approval (where required) → Authorized Work → Review / QA → Founder / CEO Release Approval → Controlled Release → Audit Record**

Approval must be explicit for high-impact or irreversible actions. A general instruction to continue does not override a specific approval gate.

## 9. Company Records and Unknowns

The following must remain marked **Not yet confirmed** until the Founder supplies authoritative information:

- Legal registered company name and registration number
- Incorporation status and jurisdictional filings
- Registered and operating addresses
- Tax registrations and statutory identifiers
- Bank accounts and authorized signatories
- Human employment and contractor records
- Customer contracts and commercial launch status

Do not invent or infer these details.

## 10. Review and Approval

This charter is a working draft. It becomes an approved internal company policy only after the Founder / CEO explicitly approves the document and its version.
