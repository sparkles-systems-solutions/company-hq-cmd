# Virtual AI Team — Capability Assessment

**Company:** Sparkles Systems & Solutions  
**Assessment batch:** 41 virtual AI roles  
**Assessment type:** Preliminary desk-based capability mapping  
**Assessment date:** 2026-11-24  
**Assessor:** ChatGPT-assisted role analysis; not an independent agent or human assessor  
**Status:** Preliminary assessment documented; practical workflow validation pending

## 1. Purpose and assessment boundary

This document maps each registered virtual AI role to its expected work, suitable AI-assisted capabilities, evidence required before operational use, and non-delegable human/CEO controls.

This is a desk-based assessment derived from the role descriptions and the capabilities/limitations of the ChatGPT interaction environment. It is **not** a simulated interview, independent agent test, professional certification, employee performance review, or proof that 41 separate agents exist. The company has one human member: the Founder / CEO. All other positions are virtual AI roles.

## 2. Rating method

| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Role definition | Responsibilities and expected outputs are sufficiently described for initial planning |
| AI task suitability | Work can generally be supported by research, drafting, analysis, planning, code assistance, or structured reasoning |
| Human control | Work requires CEO decision, human execution, or qualified professional review |
| Validation evidence | A real sample workflow, review record, test result, or approved deliverable still needs to be collected |

Preliminary suitability labels:
- **High:** Most routine knowledge-work outputs can be prepared or analyzed with AI assistance, subject to review.
- **Medium:** AI can assist, but the role depends materially on external systems, context, specialist judgment, or operational execution.
- **Restricted:** AI can prepare supporting material only; qualified human authority or explicit CEO approval is essential.

No numeric pass score is assigned because no standardized practical tests have yet been run.

## 4. Cross-role competency findings

### Suitable for AI-assisted delivery
- Research, summarization, drafting, structured analysis, planning, requirements decomposition, code assistance, test design, documentation, and decision-support material.
- Preparing repeatable templates, checklists, risk registers, and status reports.
- Reviewing supplied evidence and identifying gaps, inconsistencies, and likely risks.

### Not established by this assessment
- Independent persistent agents for each role.
- Background execution, independent monitoring, or autonomous work between conversations.
- Access to company systems, email, cloud accounts, GitHub write permissions, financial accounts, or production environments unless a specific approved integration is connected and authorized.
- Real-world execution, professional licences, legal authority, human employment, or independent accountability.
- Proven role performance under production conditions.

## 5. Mandatory control gates before activation

Every role must pass the following gates before its CMD status can become Active:

1. **Role scope confirmed:** Deliverables, exclusions, reporting line, and escalation path are documented.
2. **Data classification:** Approved data types and prohibited data types are identified.
3. **Permission review:** Least-privilege access is documented; no shared credentials or broad admin access.
4. **Workflow test:** At least one representative task is completed in a safe test context and reviewed.
5. **Quality acceptance:** Output accuracy, traceability, and review requirements are confirmed by the relevant owner/CEO.
6. **Risk acceptance:** Security, privacy, legal, financial, and operational risks are recorded.
7. **CEO authorization:** CEO explicitly approves activation.
8. **Live CMD verification:** The deployed system correctly enforces role scope, approvals, audit logs, and revocation.

For dashboard access without individual login, use only scoped, read-only, expiring and revocable access tokens; apply internal network/VPN restrictions where feasible and log access. This must not become unrestricted public access.

## 6. Role-specific mandatory restrictions

- **Finance:** No bank credentials, payment execution, lending, spending, or financial commitments.
- **Legal:** No legal representation, signing, binding opinion, or claim of attorney-client relationship.
- **Cybersecurity / ICT / Cloud / DevOps / Database:** No destructive or production changes without explicit CEO approval, tested backups, change record, and rollback plan.
- **HR:** No human recruitment, employment, disciplinary, or personal-data decisions by AI.
- **QA/UAT:** AI-generated test plans are not test execution evidence; business acceptance must come from an authorized human.
- **Customer Support:** No contractual promises, refunds, or disclosure of confidential information without authorization.
