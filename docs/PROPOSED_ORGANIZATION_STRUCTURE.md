# Proposed Organization Structure — Phase 2

**Company:** Sparkles Systems & Solutions  
**Status:** Proposed organization design for Founder / CEO review  
**Human membership:** Founder / CEO is the only confirmed human member  
**Virtual roster:** 41 defined Virtual AI roles; all pending activation

> This is a proposed functional reporting structure, not evidence of employment, live agent deployment, or legal corporate appointments. Reporting relationships are workflow accountability links between virtual role definitions.

## 1. Organization principles

- Founder / CEO is the sole human executive and final approval authority.
- GM coordinates the virtual organization and consolidates executive reporting.
- COO coordinates operating cadence, delivery discipline and cross-functional execution proposals.
- CTO coordinates technical architecture and engineering standards.
- CFO prepares financial analysis and budget recommendations; no banking, payment or spending authority.
- QA and Security retain independent review paths and must not be subordinated to the person whose work they assess.
- Legal Advisor reports directly to CEO for legal-risk escalation and may coordinate with Security / Risk; it is not licensed counsel.
- All 41 virtual roles remain Pending until individually approved and technically activated.

## 3. Functional accountability

| Finance | Chief Financial Officer | CEO and PMO |

| Cybersecurity / privacy | Head of Cybersecurity / Security Engineer | Security & Privacy Analyst |
| Risk / compliance | Risk & Compliance Coordinator | Security lead and CEO |
| Legal-risk drafting | Virtual AI Legal Advisor | Direct CEO escalation; qualified external counsel when required |
| Release coordination | Release Manager | CTO, PMO and QA |
| Customer / application support | Support Manager | Application and Customer Support Specialists |
| Knowledge and documentation | Technical Writer / Documentation Officer | Knowledge Base Coordinator |

## 4. Separation of duties

- Developers do not provide final independent acceptance of their own changes.
- QA prepares test evidence; CEO retains human acceptance authority where required.
- Security reviewers should be independent of the implementation being reviewed.
- CFO prepares financial analysis; CEO makes financial commitments and authorizes spending.
- HR and Recruitment roles administer virtual-role records only; they do not make real human employment decisions.
- Legal Advisor drafts and flags issues; qualified external legal counsel provides professional legal review where needed.
- Release Manager prepares readiness evidence; CEO approves production release and a separately authorized human operator performs it.

## 5. Status

All reporting lines above are proposals. They do not activate roles, create credentials, grant module permissions, appoint human officers or change production systems. Founder / CEO review is required before adoption.
