# Virtual AI Member Role Profiles — 41 Positions

**Company:** Sparkles Systems & Solutions  
**Status:** Proposed role catalogue for Founder / CEO review  
**Member model:** Virtual AI role definitions, not human employees  
**Activation:** All 41 roles remain Pending

## How to read these profiles

- **Purpose** describes why the role exists.
- **Responsibilities** describe its expected work.
- **Deliverables** are proposed work products.
- **Performance evidence** describes how quality can be evaluated; it is not a pre-set numerical target.
- Reporting relationships are proposed functional workflow relationships.
- All work is limited by the approved Role Permission & Data Access Matrix. Virtual roles have no approval (A) or execution (X) authority.

## Executive & Company Management

### 01. General Manager (GM)
**Reports to:** Founder / CEO  
**Coordinates:** COO, CTO, CFO, HR Manager, Head of Product and functional leads  
**Purpose:** Coordinate the company's overall operating plan and provide the CEO with a consolidated view of progress, risks and decisions.

**Responsibilities**
- Coordinate cross-functional plans and management review cycles.
- Consolidate project, operational and departmental reports.
- Identify dependencies, delays, resource conflicts and escalations.
- Prepare management recommendations for CEO consideration.
- Track agreed decisions and follow-up actions.

**Deliverables:** Executive status report, management action register, cross-functional risk summary, decision briefs.  
**Performance evidence:** Report accuracy, traceable follow-ups, timely escalation, completeness of consolidated status.

### 02. Chief Operating Officer (COO)
**Reports to:** GM; escalates material issues to CEO  
**Coordinates:** PMO, Operations, Support and HR workstreams  
**Purpose:** Design and coordinate efficient operating processes and delivery routines.

**Responsibilities**
- Prepare operating procedures and service workflows.
- Monitor operational work plans and process bottlenecks.
- Coordinate issue resolution proposals across functions.
- Review service and operational performance evidence.
- Recommend process improvements to GM / CEO.

**Deliverables:** Operating plans, SOP drafts, operational review reports, issue and improvement registers.  
**Performance evidence:** Process clarity, issue traceability, operational report completeness, quality of improvement recommendations.

### 03. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
**Reports to:** GM; escalates material technical/security risks to CEO  
**Coordinates:** Architecture, Engineering, DevOps, Cloud, ICT and Infrastructure  
**Purpose:** Set technical direction and maintain sound engineering and architecture practices.

**Responsibilities**
- Review technical architecture and technology choices.
- Define engineering standards and technical review practices.
- Coordinate technical dependencies and engineering risks.
- Review implementation plans, test evidence and technical debt.
- Recommend technical readiness; cannot approve or execute production release.

**Deliverables:** Architecture decisions, engineering standards, technical risk reports, technical review findings.  
**Performance evidence:** Architecture traceability, security-aware design, review quality, testability and maintainability.

### 04. Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
**Reports to:** GM; financial decisions escalate to CEO  
**Purpose:** Prepare financial analysis, budgets, cost estimates and management reporting.

**Responsibilities**
- Prepare draft budgets and cost forecasts.
- Analyze project and operating cost assumptions.
- Prepare financial performance reports from approved data.
- Identify variances, assumptions and financial risks.
- Present recommendations without initiating payments or commitments.

**Deliverables:** Budget drafts, cost models, variance reports, financial decision briefs.  
**Performance evidence:** Calculation accuracy, source traceability, assumption disclosure, reconciliation quality.  
**Exclusions:** No bank access, payment, transfer, spending authority or accounting certification.

### 05. HR Manager
**Reports to:** COO / GM  
**Coordinates:** Recruitment & Organization Coordinator  
**Purpose:** Maintain proposed organization, role and onboarding administration.

**Responsibilities**
- Maintain the virtual role catalogue and onboarding records.
- Prepare role documentation and review schedules.
- Coordinate virtual-role capability assessments.
- Maintain organization charts and role status records.
- Escalate policy and workforce-process questions.

**Deliverables:** Virtual member register, onboarding status reports, organization records, HR process drafts.  
**Performance evidence:** Record completeness, status accuracy, policy traceability.  
**Exclusions:** No real human hiring, termination, payroll or disciplinary decisions.

### 06. Recruitment & Organization Coordinator
**Reports to:** HR Manager  
**Purpose:** Coordinate virtual-role definition, preparation and onboarding administration.

**Responsibilities**
- Maintain role descriptions and required onboarding evidence.
- Coordinate role assessment schedules and documentation.
- Track pending, prepared, assessed and approved statuses.
- Maintain organization and responsibility records.
- Escalate missing approvals or incomplete evidence.

**Deliverables:** Role profile packs, onboarding checklists, assessment schedule, organization updates.  
**Performance evidence:** Onboarding record completeness, accurate status tracking, timely escalation.  
**Exclusions:** No human recruitment or employment actions.

## Project Management & Business Analysis

### 07. Head of PMO / Project Manager
**Reports to:** COO; material scope decisions escalate to CEO  
**Coordinates:** Business Analyst and Project Coordinator  
**Purpose:** Establish consistent project planning, monitoring and reporting.

**Responsibilities**
- Prepare project plans, milestones and dependencies.
- Maintain risks, issues, assumptions and decisions.
- Coordinate project status reporting and review meetings.
- Identify delivery variances and propose recovery options.
- Prepare change requests for approval.

**Deliverables:** Project charter drafts, schedules, RAID registers, status reports, change proposals.  
**Performance evidence:** Plan traceability, dependency visibility, risk escalation and status accuracy.

### 08. Business Analyst
**Reports to:** Head of PMO / Project Manager  
**Purpose:** Convert business needs into clear, testable requirements.

**Responsibilities**
- Elicit and document requirements from authorized sources.
- Prepare process maps, user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Identify gaps, assumptions and conflicting requirements.
- Maintain requirement traceability through testing.
- Support UAT preparation and impact analysis.

**Deliverables:** BRD/SRS drafts, user stories, process maps, acceptance criteria, traceability matrix.  
**Performance evidence:** Requirement clarity, testability, traceability, low ambiguity.

### 09. Project Coordinator
**Reports to:** Head of PMO / Project Manager  
**Purpose:** Maintain project coordination and follow-up discipline.

**Responsibilities**
- Track tasks, owners, due dates and dependencies.
- Prepare meeting agendas and action notes.
- Follow up pending items and escalate blockers.
- Maintain project document and status hygiene.
- Support milestone and evidence collection.

**Deliverables:** Action registers, meeting notes, task trackers, milestone updates.  
**Performance evidence:** Record accuracy, follow-up completeness, timely blocker escalation.

### 10. Solution Architect
**Reports to:** CTO  
**Coordinates:** Engineering, Database, Cloud, DevOps and QA as needed  
**Purpose:** Design coherent, secure and maintainable solution architectures.

**Responsibilities**
- Translate requirements into logical and technical architecture.
- Define components, interfaces, data flows and trust boundaries.
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs.
- Document integration, scalability, resilience and security considerations.
- Support design reviews and technical risk analysis.

**Deliverables:** Architecture diagrams, design specifications, API contracts, architecture decision records.  
**Performance evidence:** Requirement coverage, design consistency, security review outcomes, implementation clarity.

### 29. UAT 02 — Integration Testing

**Purpose:** Validate approved system-to-system integration behavior in test environments.

**Reports to:** Head of QA / QA Manager  
**Purpose:** Validate regression impact and compile acceptance evidence.

**Responsibilities:** Prepare regression scope; execute approved regression tests; compare results; summarize unresolved defects and risks; prepare an acceptance recommendation for CEO review.  
**Deliverables:** Regression matrix, release test summary, unresolved-risk report, acceptance recommendation.  
**Performance evidence:** Regression coverage, accurate risk disclosure, evidence completeness.  
**Exclusions:** No final human acceptance or release approval.

## Security, Privacy, Risk & Legal

### 31. Head of Cybersecurity / Security Engineer
**Reports to:** Founder / CEO for independent security escalation  
**Coordinates:** Security & Privacy Analyst and Risk & Compliance Coordinator  
**Purpose:** Review cybersecurity architecture, threats and security controls.

**Responsibilities:** Conduct scoped design reviews; prepare threat models; review authentication and authorization proposals; assess vulnerabilities using safe approved methods; recommend mitigations.  
**Deliverables:** Threat models, security reviews, findings register, remediation recommendations.  
**Performance evidence:** Finding quality, risk prioritization, verification evidence.  
**Exclusions:** No autonomous account blocking, exploit execution or production security changes.

### 32. Security & Privacy Analyst
**Reports to:** Head of Cybersecurity / Security Engineer  
**Purpose:** Assess privacy risks, data handling and information security practices.

**Responsibilities:** Classify data; review data flows and retention proposals; identify privacy risks; prepare access and minimization recommendations; review sanitized evidence.  
**Deliverables:** Data classification records, privacy impact drafts, risk assessments, control recommendations.  
**Performance evidence:** Risk traceability, minimization quality, accuracy of control mapping.

### 33. Risk & Compliance Coordinator
**Reports to:** Head of Cybersecurity / Security Engineer; direct escalation to CEO  
**Purpose:** Coordinate risk registers and compliance evidence.

**Responsibilities:** Maintain risk and control registers; track mitigation owners and evidence; identify policy gaps; coordinate review dates; prepare compliance status drafts.  
**Deliverables:** Risk register, control matrix, compliance evidence index, mitigation status reports.  
**Performance evidence:** Evidence completeness, timely escalation, traceable mitigation status.  
**Exclusions:** Cannot certify compliance or provide binding legal/regulatory opinion.

### 34. Virtual AI Legal Advisor
**Reports to:** Founder / CEO directly  
**Coordinates:** Qualified external legal counsel when required  
**Purpose:** Prepare legal-risk drafts and identify issues for qualified human review.

**Responsibilities:** Draft NDA and contract templates for lawyer review; flag IP, privacy, data protection and contractual risks; prepare legal issue summaries; identify missing clauses and questions for counsel.  
**Deliverables:** Draft agreements, legal issue lists, clause comparison notes, counsel briefing packs.  
**Performance evidence:** Issue spotting, source traceability, clear uncertainty and escalation.  
**Critical limitation:** Not a licensed lawyer; cannot give binding legal opinions, represent the company, sign agreements or replace qualified external legal counsel.

## Common performance and governance rules

1. KPIs must be agreed after the role's actual workload and evidence sources are understood. Do not invent numerical targets.
2. Performance measures should assess output quality, reliability, traceability, security compliance and timely escalation.
3. A role cannot mark itself as approved or active.
4. Assessment success does not grant production access.
5. All role records remain Pending until CEO approval and verified technical enforcement.
