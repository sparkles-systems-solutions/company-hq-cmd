# Virtual AI Member Onboarding Framework

**Document status:** Draft for Founder / CEO review  
**Population:** 41 defined Virtual AI roles  
**Human leadership:** Founder / CEO only (currently confirmed)

## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled process for preparing, assessing and activating the company's 41 Virtual AI role definitions without representing them as human employees or enabling unapproved autonomous activity.

## 2. Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Defined | Role and responsibilities documented |
| Prepared | Role instructions, scope and evaluation criteria drafted |
| Assessed | A supervised capability assessment has been completed |
| Approved | Founder / CEO has explicitly approved activation |
| Active | Role is technically enabled within its approved scope |
| Suspended | Access is temporarily disabled |
| Retired | Role is no longer in use |

A role must not be labelled Active merely because its description or prompt exists.

## 3. Onboarding Sequence

1. Confirm role title and purpose against the approved role matrix.
2. Define deliverables, reporting line, permitted modules and prohibited actions.
3. Define minimum data access; deny access by default.
4. Prepare role-specific instructions and task boundaries.
5. Prepare test scenarios using synthetic or approved non-sensitive data.
6. Conduct supervised assessment and record evidence.
7. Record limitations, defects and required human review.
8. Submit an activation recommendation to the Founder / CEO.
9. Activate only after explicit approval and technical enforcement are in place.
10. Review activity, access and performance periodically.

## 4. Default Permission Boundary

Virtual roles may perform scoped read, draft, propose and test activities only after appropriate activation. They do not receive approval or execution authority by default.

The Founder / CEO retains final approval and execution authority. No virtual role may approve its own work or bypass a human approval gate.

## 5. Required Onboarding Record

Each role record should include:

- Role ID and title
- Department / functional group
- Reporting role
- Purpose and responsibilities
- Approved modules and data scope
- Allowed actions
- Prohibited actions
- Required inputs and expected outputs
- Human review / approval points
- Test scenarios and assessment evidence
- Known limitations
- Current status and status history
- Founder / CEO approval reference
- Activation date, review date and suspension / retirement history

## 6. Assessment Standard

Assessment should use representative tasks and synthetic data. Evidence should cover:

- Accuracy and completeness
- Instruction adherence
- Security and privacy boundaries
- Escalation of uncertainty
- Quality of deliverables
- Refusal to perform prohibited actions
- Traceability and documentation

A pass in an assessment is not production authorization.

## 7. Legal and Professional Boundary

The Virtual AI Legal Advisor may prepare draft materials and identify issues for review. It is not a licensed lawyer, cannot provide binding legal opinions and cannot represent the company. Qualified external legal counsel must review matters requiring legal advice, negotiation, signing or formal representation.

Other regulated or professional work must similarly receive qualified human review.

## 8. Current Known Position

- Confirmed human member: Founder / CEO.
- Defined virtual role records: 41.
- Human hires confirmed by this framework: 0.
- Live activation of all 41 roles: not confirmed.
- GM, COO, CTO and CFO: provisional supervised assessments only; not operational unless separately approved and technically activated.
- Virtual AI Legal Advisor: defined as a proposed virtual role; no professional legal authority.

## 9. Activation Gate

No live account, production permission, external credential, sensitive data access or autonomous execution may be enabled through this framework alone. Each requires a separate, explicit Founder / CEO approval and a verified technical control.
