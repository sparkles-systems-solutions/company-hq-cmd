# Company Organization & Virtual AI Recruitment Register

**Company:** Sparkles Systems & Solutions

## 1. Company membership policy

The Founder / CEO is the company's only human member. All other organizational positions are virtual AI roles. A role being listed or assigned does not mean a real person has been hired. Virtual AI role assignments do not create employment relationships or professional licences.

## 2. Recruitment status definitions

- **CEO-confirmed (Human):** The Founder / CEO role.
- **Virtual role assigned:** The role has been designated for virtual AI support in prior company planning. This is not a human appointment.
- **Role registered in proposed migration:** A role/member seed is present in a GitHub SQL migration, but it is not active in a live CMD database until the migration is applied and verified.
- **Role defined — activation pending:** Position and responsibilities are defined; a dedicated virtual workflow/member record has not yet been verified.
- **Human professional review required:** AI may prepare drafts and analysis, but a suitably qualified human professional must review or act where required.

## 5. Recruitment and activation workflow

For each virtual role, HR should maintain these separate checkpoints:

1. Position approved and job description defined.
2. Role-specific capability assessment / simulated interview completed, where useful.
3. CEO accepts the virtual role assignment.
4. Role profile and reporting line recorded.
5. Access scope and permissions reviewed (least privilege).
6. Role registered in CMD.
7. Role workflow and deliverables tested.
8. Status changed to **Active** only after the required checks are complete.

No simulated interview, appointment, database registration, or test may be reported as completed unless evidence exists.

## 7. CEO authority and safety

The CEO retains final authority. AI members may research, plan, draft, analyze, and recommend. They cannot independently hire humans, spend money, sign contracts, deploy to production, change production data, make irreversible changes, or bind the company. Those actions require explicit CEO approval and, where applicable, qualified human professional review.
